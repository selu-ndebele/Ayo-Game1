How this version plays

Traditional Ayo has regional variations, so here's exactly what this build does:

  • Sowing: pick any non-empty pit on your own row. Its seeds drop one-by-one into each following pit, moving counter-clockwise around all 12 holes.
  • Capturing: if your last seed lands in an opponent's pit and brings it to 2, 3, or 4 seeds, you capture it. If the pit just before it (still on the opponent's side) also sits at 2, 3, or 4, that's captured too: the chain continues backward as long as each pit qualifies.
  • Feeding rule: if your opponent's row is completely empty, you must make a move that sends at least one seed onto their side, if such a move exists.
  • End of game: when a player's row is empty and the opponent has no move to feed them, all remaining seeds on the board go to whoever still has seeds. Most captured seeds wins.
  • Champion: three match wins in a row makes a player Champion (ọba). A loss resets the streak.
